# Texas counties in the hosted pack of 2026-11-13

Counts for the two Texas counties added to the hosted registry with the pack of 2026-11-13, read from the pack's registry file (`tx.db`) and its manifest. Each county's source record is its config in `registry/sources/tx/` and its page in this folder ([Bell](tx-bell-cad.md), [Johnson](tx-johnson-cad.md)). "Organisation owner" counts parcels whose owner of record is an organisation; natural persons' names are never stored.

## Counties

- Bell County (`tx-bell-cad`): 13,685 commercial parcels; 7,224 with an organisation owner of record; data as of 2026-10-01; roll years of values: 2024 to 2026.
- Johnson County (`tx-johnson-cad`): 4,416 commercial parcels; 2,868 with an organisation owner of record; data as of 2026-08-10; roll years of values: 2024 to 2026.

Both districts' rolls are public record (Tex. Gov't Code ch. 552) with no licence or terms of use stated; the 2024 and 2025 rolls are loaded as value history only. Neither county has building records in the pack (its building count is 0); the parcels carry their own building facts, such as year built and improvement area, where the roll gives them.
