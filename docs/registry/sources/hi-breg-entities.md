## Publisher and URLs

- Publisher: Hawaii DCCA, Business Registration Division (BREG), 335 Merchant Street, Room 201, Honolulu; breg@dcca.hawaii.gov, 808-586-2727. The public register is
  searched through Hawaii Business Express (<https://hbe.dcca.hawaii.gov/>), an interactive application. BREG's contact page announces a new online portal on 2026-07-06
  (as reported by search results; not re-read).
- BREG's overview says it offers "special subscription services for frequent filers and bulk downloads of information through its online services" (search-result
  summary of cca.hawaii.gov/breg/?p=297; the page was not opened). No product name, price or format was found.
- The only open file is a stale copy: the City and County of Honolulu's open data portal, dataset `9k54-ztb8` "Hawaii Business Registration"
  (<https://data.honolulu.gov/api/views/9k54-ztb8.json>), published by the Managing Director's Office, **last updated 2022-06-08**, with the note "This dataset is out of
  date. Current business registration can be viewed at the Hawaii Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs at https://hbe.dcca.hawaii.gov/". 32 text columns
  (File No., Name, Master Name, Business Type, Status, Place Incorporated, Registration Date, Mailing Address, Cross Reference Name, Term, Agent Name, Agent Address,
  Rectype, Trade Name, Certificate Number, Category, Expiration Date, Registrant, ...). It has no license field.
