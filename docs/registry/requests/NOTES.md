# Tier 1 gates: verbatim terms, statutes and forms captured 2026-11-02

Working notes for the founder, who is clearing the gates in
[BACKLOG section E](../../BACKLOG.md) personally. Everything below was read in the desktop app's
built-in browser on 2026-11-02 unless a line says otherwise; quotations are verbatim from the page
at the URL given. Nothing was signed, paid for, accepted, sent or logged into. Where a page was
behind a bot challenge or refused to load, that is recorded and the URL is left for a person to open.
No memo, letter, request or report has been written; the forms the brief names are in
[`forms/`](forms/) with their source URLs listed at the end.

  previous years or prior rolls from the current year are available by request."

- Statute, s. 119.01(1), F.S. (2026),
  <https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0100-0199/0119/Sections/0119.01.html>:

  > It is the policy of this state that all state, county, and municipal records are open for
  > personal inspection and copying by any person. Providing access to public records is a duty of
  > each agency.

  s. 119.01(2)(a): "Automation of public records must not erode the right of access to those
  records." s. 119.01(2)(f): an agency "must provide a copy of the record in the medium requested if
  the agency maintains the record in that medium, and the agency may charge a fee in accordance with
  this chapter."

- Statute, s. 119.07(1)(a), F.S. (2026),
  <https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0100-0199/0119/Sections/0119.07.html>:

  > Every person who has custody of a public record shall permit the record to be inspected and
  > copied by any person desiring to do so, at any reasonable time, under reasonable conditions,
  > and under supervision by the custodian of the public records.

  s. 119.07(4) sets copy fees (up to 15 cents per page, otherwise "the actual cost of duplication",
  plus a "special service charge" under (4)(d) where a request requires extensive use of
  information technology resources). Neither section read says anything about commercial use or
  redistribution of records once obtained.
