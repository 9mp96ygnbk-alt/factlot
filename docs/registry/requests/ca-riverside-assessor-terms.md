## What the statute says

Gov. Code § 7922.530(a) (read 2026-11-02, NOTES.md; <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov>): each state or local agency, upon a request for a copy of records that reasonably describes an identifiable record, "shall make the records promptly available to any person upon payment of fees covering direct costs of duplication, or a statutory fee if applicable." The opening of that sentence excepts records exempt by express provisions of law.

Rev. & Tax. Code § 408.3(a) (read 2026-11-02): "property characteristics information maintained by the assessor is a public record and shall be open to public inspection." The capture's list of characteristics includes use code designations. The assessor "may require that a fee reasonably related to the actual cost of developing and providing the information be paid", and that cost "may include recovery of developmental and indirect costs".

Rev. & Tax. Code § 408(a) (read 2026-11-02) withholds records "not required by law to be kept or prepared by the assessor". The capture reads the assessment roll as not restricted by that sentence. Commercial use: silent. Redistribution: silent. Gov. Code § 7921.000 is NOT CONFIRMED. On 2026-11-03 the leginfo section URL returned HTTP 403, Cloudflare "Just a moment..." (see `BLOCKED.md`).

## Options

1. Use as a public record.
2. Ask the publisher for a written statement (letter: `ca-riverside-assessor-letter.md`).
3. Do not load.

## Recommendation

Choose option 2 for the free CREST tables. Do not load them, and do not order the paid roll. "Public Use" does not define redistribution. The statutes read make the records available and allow a characteristics fee. They do not say a recipient may republish the fields.

Address the Riverside County Assessor at the post-office box above. Ask whether the county objects if FactLot publishes derived facts about commercial parcels, cited back to the CREST General Table (and the tax-year table), with no natural-person names stored. The free tables, as described in the review record, do not carry owner names. Leave `verified` false until the written answer. The paid files stay behind their own agreement and are not part of this recommendation.

## Risk if wrong

If "Public Use" means any public use, including a cited open registry, then waiting delays a free class-and-value table. If "Public Use" means the public may look and the county still owns the reuse decision, then loading now publishes under a licence that never grants redistribution. The unpaid Data Distribution Agreement is a separate risk: using the paid owner-bearing files without it is outside this memo and should not be done to fill the owner gap. A wrong read of two words is the whole gate.
