# Tyler / Socrata terms of service

Final URL: https://www.tylertech.com/terms/socrata-terms-of-service
HTTP 200. Read 2026-11-03.
Status versus an earlier browser capture: VERIFIED for the restrictions compared below. The page is Tyler's general Terms of Use. It is marked "Effective April 29, 2024".

This memo is research, not legal advice.

> Effective April 29, 2024

> Use any computer code, data mining software, "robot," "bot," "spider," "scraper" or other automatic device, or program, algorithm or methodology having similar processes or functionality, or any manual process, to monitor or copy any of the Web pages, data or content found or accessed through the Services;

> Display, distribute, license, perform, publish, reproduce, duplicate, copy, create derivative works from, modify, sell, resell, exploit, transfer, or transmit for any commercial purposes, all or any portion of the Services

These sentences restrict the Tyler "Services." They ban automated copying of pages, data, or content found through the Services, and they ban commercial display, distribution, publication, reproduction, derivative works, and resale of any portion of the Services without Tyler's permission. If a government dataset is treated as part of those Services, the clause conflicts with redistribution and with automated collection. The page does not say that a public agency's own open-data licence is overridden. Several review records rely on the agency's terms instead. This fetch does not replace those agency terms.

Review records that identify a Socrata portal: `il-cook-assessor` (datacatalog.cookcountyil.gov); `ny-nyc-dof` (data.cityofnewyork.us); `ny-dos-entities` (data.ny.gov); `pa-sos-entities` (data.pa.gov); `co-sos-entities` (data.colorado.gov); `wa-sos-entities` (data.wa.gov). `ca-san-francisco-assessor` is DataSF on Socrata and is covered in `datasf.md`. `wa-king-assessor` notes that data.kingcounty.gov is a Socrata portal and is not the source file. `ca-los-angeles-assessor` says the portal is no longer Socrata. `ca-alameda-assessor` says the hub is ArcGIS, not Socrata. `docs/registry/texas-sources.md` says the Comptroller portal's Socrata terms still need a human read. This page is that platform text.
