> Click-Through Master Agreement

> Revised August 1, 2025

> Grants to Customer a nonexclusive, nontransferable right and license or subscription to access and use Esri

> Authorizes Customer to copy and make derivative works of the Documentation for Customer

> means a license to reproduce and distribute Software provided that

> Customer does not charge a fee to others for the use of the Software.

> Probes, scans, or tests the vulnerability of Cloud Services or breach any security or authentication measures

> Benchmarks the availability, performance, or functionality of Cloud Services.

E204 is Esri's licence to its customer for Esri Offerings. The grant is nonexclusive and nontransferable. Derivative works of the Documentation are for the customer's own internal use. A Redistribution License, where one is granted, is a licence to reproduce and distribute Software under conditions that include not charging a fee for use of the Software. B.8.1 forbids probing Cloud Services without written approval and forbids benchmarking those services.

This agreement does not, by its own terms, forbid a member of the public from redistributing a county file the county published. It binds a customer who accepts it. FactLot is not signing it. It would matter if FactLot were the Esri customer using Esri software or cloud services to run the registry.
