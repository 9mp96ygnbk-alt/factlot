# Task 5: ArcGIS Hub and county open-data downloads

Read 2026-11-03.

This memo is research, not legal advice; the founder holds no attorney or real estate license.

No separate public page of ArcGIS Hub terms for downloaders was in the task5-raw capture, and none was found on the Esri pages fetched. The pages fetched are the master-agreement index (https://www.esri.com/en-us/legal/terms/master-agreement), the product-specific scope page (https://www.esri.com/en-us/legal/terms/product-specific-scope-of-use), E204CW, and E300. The scope page's English E300 link is https://www.esri.com/content/dam/esrisites/en-us/media/legal/product-specific-terms-of-use/e300.pdf .

Hub, inside E300 footnote 85, is a limit on the customer's included software licences. See `esri-e300.md`. E204 is the customer's master agreement. See `esri-e204.md`. Neither document is a licence of a county's public records to an anonymous downloader.

Status: the capture correctly recorded that no separate Hub downloader terms were opened. This read does not find one either. NOT CONFIRMED that every county hub is silent; a county can publish its own terms. Los Angeles County's review record names https://egis-lacounty.hub.arcgis.com/pages/terms-of-use . That county page was not fetched.

Review records that use an ArcGIS Hub or an opendata.arcgis.com site: `az-maricopa-assessor`, `ca-los-angeles-assessor`, `ca-alameda-assessor`, `ca-orange-assessor`, `ca-riverside-assessor`, `va-fairfax-assessor`, `mn-hennepin-assessor`, `ga-fulton-assessor`, `dc-otr-real-property`, `dc-dlcp-entities`, `mi-wayne-parcels`, `co-denver-assessor`, `nv-clark-assessor`, `tx-collin-cad`, and the GIS note in `wa-king-assessor`. District of Columbia catalog data is governed by the DC terms in `dc-district-data.md`, which allow redistribution of that data. A county's own terms, where the review record says they are narrower, are not erased by E300.

E300 and E204 do not restrict redistribution of a public record a county has published to the public. They restrict the Esri customer. FactLot should not create an Esri account or accept either agreement in order to download a county file.
