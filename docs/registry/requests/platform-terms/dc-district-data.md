> You can copy, modify, distribute, and perform the data, even for commercial purposes, all without asking permission.

> Data retrieved from Open Data DC catalog

> (https://opendata.dc.gov)

> Unless otherwise noted, the data on the Sites is public domain and made available with a Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal dedication.

The page limits these terms to non-copyrightable data from the District Data Catalog and says they do not apply to other dc.gov content. Logos are excluded from the CC0 dedication and need written permission. Copyrighted or trademarked material on the sites keeps that protection.

These terms do not restrict redistribution of catalog data. They expressly allow copy, modification, distribution, and performance, including for commercial purposes, without asking permission. The requested attribution is "Data retrieved from Open Data DC catalog" with https://opendata.dc.gov.

Review records: `dc-otr-real-property` and `dc-dlcp-entities`. Both describe Open Data DC datasets whose downloads redirect to hub.arcgis.com. The District data terms above are the publisher's terms for that catalog data. Esri's customer licence is a separate document and is not these terms.
