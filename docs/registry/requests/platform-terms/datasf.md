# DataSF terms of use

Final URL: https://www.sf.gov/reports--april-2017--datasf-terms-use
HTTP 200. Read 2026-11-03.
Status versus an earlier browser capture: VERIFIED for the operative clauses compared below. The capture wraps the same sentences across lines. The licence, acceptance-by-download, derivative-work, re-identification, and indemnity wording matches.

This memo is research, not legal advice.

> Downloading or using any of the Data or any Derivative Work, in which case you understand and agree that the City will treat your download or use of the Data or a Derivative Work as an acceptance of the Terms of Use from that point forward.

> "Derivative Work" means a work that is based in any way or to any extent on the Data including without limitation any work that uses any of the Data in a modified form.

> You hereby understand and agree that You will not take any measures to re-identify the Data or to contact individuals whose information is represented by the Data.

> from Your use of the Data, including without limitation Your use of the Data in a Derivative Work.

> Except where otherwise stated in the file containing such Data or on the page from which such Data is accessed, including its metadata, Data is made available under the Public Domain Dedication and License v1.0 whose full text can be found at: http://www.opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1.0/

The page dated on its face "Friday, April 21, 2017" dedicates Data to the Public Domain Dedication and License v1.0 except where a file or its metadata says otherwise. That dedication does not restrict redistribution of the data. The terms still bind by download or use, forbid re-identification, and require indemnity for use of the data in a derivative work. They are not a ban on publishing cited facts. A file-level exception could narrow a particular dataset. None was read here.
