# San Bernardino County Assessor property data files (`ca-san-bernardino-assessor`): terms decision memo

Date read: 2026-11-03 (earlier captures 2026-11-02 as marked). Prepared for: the founder.
This memo is research, not legal advice; the founder holds no attorney or real estate license.

## What the publisher's terms say

The files are the "Assessor Property Data Files" on <https://arc.sbcounty.gov/property-information/> (read 2026-11-02, NOTES.md): secured roll, unsecured roll, parcel maps, property characteristics, and related files. The page states: "Names and address information protected per California Government Code Section 7928.205." The text of § 7928.205 was not read, so its reach is NOT CONFIRMED beyond that sentence. The review record says the secured-roll layout marks name, situs, and mailing-address fields hidden. This lane did not open a roll file.

The page's "LIMITATION OF LIABILITY" (read 2026-11-02, NOTES.md) says:

> Data provided by the San Bernardino County Assessor's Office is maintained for internal use only in the determination of property value for the purpose of taxation.

The same paragraph says the office "makes no claims, promises, or guarantees about the accuracy, completeness, or adequacy of the contents of the files." and "No warranty of any kind, expressed or implied, including but not limited to, the implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for particular purposes is made." No sentence in the capture grants redistribution or mentions AI use. "maintained for internal use only" can be read as the office's purpose, or as a limit on the recipient. The capture does not resolve which reading is right.

<https://main.sbcounty.gov/policy/>, as captured on 2026-11-02, showed section headings only, including "Information Disclaimer," and no licence text. This lane did not re-fetch it.

Office address, read 2026-11-03 from <https://arc.sbcounty.gov/san-bernardino-assessor-district-office/>: "222 W. Hospitality Lane", "3rd Floor", "San Bernardino, CA 92415", phone 909.386.8870. The page does not print an email address.

## What the statute says

Gov. Code § 7922.530(a) (read 2026-11-02, NOTES.md): on a request that reasonably describes an identifiable record, the agency "shall make the records promptly available to any person upon payment of fees covering direct costs of duplication, or a statutory fee if applicable." The opening of that sentence excepts records exempt by express provisions of law.

Rev. & Tax. Code § 408.3(a) (read 2026-11-02): "property characteristics information maintained by the assessor is a public record and shall be open to public inspection." Use codes, square footage, acreage, and multifamily unit counts are in the capture's list of characteristics. A fee "reasonably related to the actual cost" may be charged, and that cost "may include recovery of developmental and indirect costs".

Rev. & Tax. Code § 408(a) (read 2026-11-02) withholds records "not required by law to be kept or prepared by the assessor". The capture reads the assessment roll as not restricted by that sentence. Commercial use: silent. Redistribution: silent. Gov. Code § 7921.000 is NOT CONFIRMED (HTTP 403, Cloudflare, on 2026-11-03; see `BLOCKED.md`).

## Options

1. Use as a public record.
2. Ask the publisher for a written statement (letter: `ca-san-bernardino-assessor-letter.md`).
3. Do not load.

## Recommendation

Choose option 2, and do not flip `verified` until the Assessor answers. The files are ordinary downloads, with no fee and no click-through recorded in the review, and the liability paragraph is mostly a warranty disclaimer. The words "internal use only" are still a use sentence, and no licence grants redistribution. Ask the San Bernardino County Assessor, at the district-office address above, whether the county objects if FactLot publishes derived facts about commercial parcels from the non-withheld fields (parcel number, use, values, acreage), each fact cited to the file, with no natural-person name stored. Do not ask for the name and address fields the page says are protected.

## Risk if wrong

If "internal use only" is a restriction, publishing the roll is a use the page does not allow. If it only states the office's purpose and the rest is a warranty disclaimer, then waiting delays a free county file that already drops owner names and situs. Loading the hidden name columns, or trying to fill them from another vendor named on the page, would break both the page's withholding line and the registry rule against natural-person names. The letter keeps the request on the fields the page already shows.
