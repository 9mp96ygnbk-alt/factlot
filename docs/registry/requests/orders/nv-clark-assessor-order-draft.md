Subject: Ongoing subscription to the Clark County Assessor Data Site

FactLot publishes cited facts about commercial real-estate parcels and the legal entities that own them. I request an ongoing subscription to the Clark County Assessor Data Site. The files requested are the Secured Tax Roll (AOEXTRACT) and the Commercial Extraction listed on the Assessor data files page. FactLot will use them to publish, for commercial parcels only, the organisation shown as owner of record, the parcel number, the situs, the land-use code, and the assessed land and improvement values. Each fact will be cited back to the Assessor file. Natural-person names will not be stored or indexed. The raw file will not be published or given to anyone else. Use is expected every month. Please confirm the fee of $300.00 per month on the fee schedule effective July 1, 2024, how the weekend replacement files are delivered, and whether this use is acceptable under the Data Notice and the AOExtract note that the information is for assessment use only.
