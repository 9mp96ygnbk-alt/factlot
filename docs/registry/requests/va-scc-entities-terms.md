## What the statute says

No Virginia statute text was fetched on 2026-11-03. The Clerk page says the office operates under the Code of Virginia and gives no section number. Inventing a bulk-data section would be wrong.

NOTES.md (read 2026-11-02) quotes the Virginia Freedom of Information Act, which is a general public-records statute, not a Clerk price list. Va. Code § 2.2-3700(B), https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title2.2/chapter37/section2.2-3700/ :

> available for inspection and copying upon request.

> The provisions of this chapter shall be

Va. Code § 2.2-3704, https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title2.2/chapter37/section2.2-3704/ :

> a public body may make reasonable charges not to exceed its actual cost incurred in

> shall provide the requester with a cost estimate if requested.

NOTES.md says the right in § 2.2-3704(A) runs to Virginia citizens and in-state media, and that a request from elsewhere relies on the custodian's discretion. The quoted portions do not mention commercial use or redistribution of a business-entity extract. On those points the statute, as read in NOTES.md, is silent. It does not set a price for a State Corporation Commission data file.

If the Virginia Freedom of Information Act citation is used as a substitute for that page, a non-citizen request may rest only on the Clerk's discretion, and the cost may be the actual cost rather than zero.
