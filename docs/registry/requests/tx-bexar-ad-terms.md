# Bexar Central Appraisal District (`tx-bexar-ad`): terms decision memo

Date read: 2026-11-03. Prepared for: the founder.

This memo is research, not legal advice; the founder holds no attorney or real estate license.

## What the publisher's terms say

The district does not publish a bulk roll. The request draft is `tx-bexar-ad-request.md`. It has not been sent.

https://bcad.org/ (HTTP 200, read 2026-11-03) links the words "Open Records Request" to https://bcad.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/BCAD-Public-Information-Act-Request.pdf (HTTP 200, read 2026-11-03). That is the preferred form (GR1000-34). The recipient block on it is Records Management Officer, Bexar Central Appraisal District, 411 North Frio Street, San Antonio, TX 78207, email OpenRecords@bcad.org. The Help Center named on the form, https://help.bcad.org/, returned HTTP 403 and "Just a moment..." (read 2026-11-03). Do not submit that site.

An older PDF is still at https://bcad.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Public_Information_Request_10-2025.pdf (read 2026-11-03). It says:

> supplemental data products exist, they are automatically uploaded for public access at no charge.

The 2026 form does not contain that sentence. Do not treat "no charge" as current. The older PDF also says "The 80th Texas Legislature added Section 552.149 to the Texas Government Code." The 2026 form does not. This request does not seek sales data. Paper rates on the 2026 form, not requested, are "Standard Size Copies* (8 ½ x 11) $0.10/each".

The request asks for the current certified appraisal roll export, commercial accounts in categories F1, F2, and B, in the FTP appraisal-data-export layout, plus the layout description. If a commercial-only cut would be a new compilation, the full real-property export is acceptable. Natural-person names will not be published. Mandatory redactions: yes. Discretionary redactions of the requested fields: no. Digital delivery only.

## What the statute says

The repository template cites Tex. Gov't Code ch. 552 and §552.2615 for an itemized estimate before charges. https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.552.htm returned HTTP 200 (read 2026-11-03) and did not render the section. The saved page contains "Texas Constitution and Statutes" and "Select Statute" and does not contain "552.2615". The statutory wording is NOT CONFIRMED. The section number is used as the template uses it. What is confirmed is the district's own poster: a written estimate when charges will exceed $40, a ten-day response or the request is treated as withdrawn, and a possible bond or deposit if the estimate exceeds $100.00. The poster is not silent on cost. It does not discuss redistribution or commercial use of a roll once released. Tax Code §25.025 is named in the repository template as confidential owner information to exclude. The section text was not fetched. The request excludes it anyway.

## Options

1. Use a published bulk file. None was found on the pages read.
2. Send the drafted request and wait for the estimate. Do not accept a non-refundable transaction until the founder agrees in writing.
3. Do not load.

## Recommendation

Option 2. The founder dates, signs, and emails the request to OpenRecords@bcad.org, or mails it to the Frio Street address. Fill the address, phone, and email placeholders first. Ask which form's FTP sentence applies. Do not pay, and do not let the district complete a transaction, until the founder accepts an itemized estimate.

## Risk if wrong

A completed electronic-data transaction is final and non-refundable. An estimate over $40 that sits unanswered for the poster's ten days is treated as withdrawn. An estimate over $100.00 can bring a bond, prepayment, or deposit. Asking for a new commercial-only compilation can add programming charges the template warned about. Taking sales data the older form ties to §552.149 would ask for a category this request deliberately leaves out.
