# Florida Department of Revenue NAL rolls (`fl-dor-nal`): terms decision memo

Date read: 2026-11-03 (earlier captures 2026-11-02 as marked). Prepared for: the founder.
This memo is research, not legal advice; the founder holds no attorney or real estate license.

## What the publisher's terms say

The dataset is the real property roll on "Download Assessment Roll and GIS Data", https://floridarevenue.com/property/Pages/DataPortal_RequestAssessmentRollGISData.aspx (read 2026-11-03). The saved page names it in this parenthetical: "real property roll (Name – Address – Legal, or NAL)".

The page and the working notes (read 2026-11-02) tie the rolls to chapter 119. The notes quote this line: "The Department of Revenue publishes assessment rolls in compliance with chapter 119, Florida". The 2026-11-03 fetch has "The Department of Revenue publishes assessment rolls in compliance with" on its own line, and the next line begins "chapter 119, Florida Statutes." The notes continue: "property owners exempt from public records disclosure under section 119.071, Florida Statutes." Prior-year rolls are by request. The request route on the fetched page is this line: "to PTOTechnology@floridarevenue.com, by fax at 850-488-9482, or by regular mail to PTO's general mailing address (Property Tax Oversight, Florida Department of Revenue, 5050 W. Tennessee St., Tallahassee, FL 32399-0100)."

Conditions of Use, https://floridarevenue.com/Pages/conditions.aspx (read 2026-11-02), section I, include these lines: "No material from MyFlorida.com or any Web site owned, operated, licensed or controlled by THE"; "STATE OF FLORIDA or DMS may be copied, reproduced, republished, uploaded, posted, transmitted,"; "or distributed in any way. Materials may be downloaded on any single personal computer, for"; "non-commercial use only providing all copyright and other proprietary notices are kept intact." The notes say the rest of that page does not mention public records, the data portal, or assessment rolls.

The privacy page, https://floridarevenue.com/Pages/privacy.aspx (read 2026-11-02), includes "Information obtained by Revenue is considered a public record by law, and it must be disclosed" and "upon request unless specifically confidential and exempt from disclosure by law."

The contact page, https://floridarevenue.com/property/Pages/contactUs.aspx (read 2026-11-03), lists "Post Office Box 3000", "Tallahassee, Florida 32315-3000", and "Telephone: 850-717-6570". "Email Your Question" is a link, and no general mailbox is printed on that page. The footer line "5050 West Tennessee Street, Tallahassee, FL 32399" is also on that page. The letter uses the portal's Property Tax Oversight mailing line. AI use is not addressed on any page read.

## What the statute says

Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, read 2026-11-02. Section 119.01(1), https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0100-0199/0119/Sections/0119.01.html: "It is the policy of this state that all state, county, and municipal records are open for" and "personal inspection and copying by any person. Providing access to public records is a duty of" and "each agency."

Section 119.07(1)(a), https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0100-0199/0119/Sections/0119.07.html: "Every person who has custody of a public record shall permit the record to be inspected and"; "copied by any person desiring to do so, at any reasonable time, under reasonable conditions,"; "and under supervision by the custodian of the public records."

The working notes state that neither section says anything about commercial use or redistribution of records once obtained. On those two sections, the statute is silent on commercial use and silent on redistribution after the copy is obtained.

## Options

1. Use as a public record.
2. Ask the publisher for a written statement (letter: `fl-dor-nal-letter.md`).
3. Do not load.

## Recommendation

Send the letter, and leave `verified` false until Property Tax Oversight answers or you decide that chapter 119 governs the roll files. The portal ties the rolls to chapter 119. The Conditions of Use say site material may not be copied or distributed and limit a download to "non-commercial use only" on one personal computer. Those sentences do not name the rolls. If that clause controls the files, publishing derived facts in an open registry is outside the use the clause describes.

## Risk if wrong

If chapter 119 controls and the registry waits, Florida commercial-parcel facts stay unpublished. If the Conditions of Use control and the registry publishes anyway, the Department has said that copying and distribution of site material violate its stated copyright terms. The review record cites a 2004 Florida Second District Court of Appeal opinion, 889 So. 2d 871. This lane did not read that opinion, so it is not quoted here.
