# Wayne County annual assessment data (`mi-wayne-parcels`): terms decision memo

Date read: 2026-11-03 (earlier captures 2026-11-02 as marked). Prepared for: the founder.
This memo is research, not legal advice; the founder holds no attorney or real estate license.

## What the publisher's terms say

The dataset is the county-wide file 2026 Wayne County Assessments, `2026-wayne-county-assessments-names-addresses-legal.zip`, linked from <https://www.waynecountymi.gov/Government/Departments/Management-Budget/Assessment-Equalization/Annual-Assessment-Data> (review record, reviewed 2026-11-02). `www.waynecountymi.gov` returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-03, so this lane did not re-fetch that page (listed in REPORT.md, "Needs a person to open"). The portal terms are the "Wayne County Terms of Use and Linking Policy" on <https://www.waynecountymi.gov/Government/About-Wayne-County/Privacy-Policy> (read 2026-11-02, NOTES.md):

> The use of the content, images and logos on the Wayne County web portal on any other web site or networked computer environment is prohibited.

> Some of the materials on the web portal may be saved on any single personal computer for personal, non-commercial use only.

> Modification of the materials or use of the materials for purposes not granted by the Wayne County is a violation of Wayne County's copyright and other proprietary rights.

The page opens with: "The use, dissemination, distribution, publication or action in reliance on any of the information contained in this communication is strictly prohibited." It cites "the Freedom of Information Act. MCL 15.231, et seq." The capture says nothing there about bulk data, automated access, or AI use.

## What the statute says

MCL 15.231(2), <https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-15-231> (read 2026-11-02):

> It is the public policy of this state that all persons, except those persons incarcerated in state or local correctional facilities, are entitled to full and complete information regarding the affairs of government and the official acts of those who represent them as public officials and public employees, consistent with this act.

MCL 15.233(1), <https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-15-233> (read 2026-11-02), states that "upon providing a public body's FOIA coordinator with a written request that describes a public record sufficiently to enable the public body to find the public record, a person has a right to inspect, copy, or receive copies of the requested public record of the public body." A request from a person other than an individual must include "the complete name, address, and contact information of the person's agent who is an individual." The same capture quotes a right to subscribe to future issuances "for up to 6 months".

Commercial use: silent. Redistribution: silent. The sections read create a right to inspect, copy, and receive copies. They do not say what the recipient may publish afterward.

## Options

1. Use as a public record.
2. Ask the publisher for a written statement (letter: `mi-wayne-parcels-letter.md`).
3. Do not load.

## Recommendation

Choose option 2, and do not load the file until the county answers in writing. The terms allow saving some materials for personal, non-commercial use only, and they prohibit use of portal content on any other website. A public registry of derived commercial-parcel facts is outside that grant. The statute is silent on reuse, so the words read do not erase the portal sentences.

Send the letter to the Wayne County FOIA coordinator for the office that publishes the file (Department of Management and Budget, Division of Assessment and Equalization). The coordinator's postal address and email are NOT CONFIRMED: the FOIA form and the procedures PDF returned HTTP 403, and <https://waynecountymi.nextrequest.com/> returned HTTP 204 with an empty body (read 2026-11-03). The review record states the division at 500 Griswold, Suite 2650, Detroit, and equalization@waynecountymi.gov, with no ZIP. Open the FOIA page, insert the live address, sign, and send. Leave `verified` false until then.

## Risk if wrong

If those sentences govern the zip, publishing derived facts on another website is a use the terms do not grant, and the page calls an ungranted use a violation of the county's copyright and other proprietary rights. If the sentences govern the portal's images, logos, and page chrome, and the posted roll is a public record, then waiting holds back a county-wide commercial file. The opening "strictly prohibited" sentence is broader than the terms section under it. A written answer is what resolves which sentence controls.
