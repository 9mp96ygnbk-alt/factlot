# Fairfax County real estate assessment tables (`va-fairfax-assessor`): terms decision memo

Date read: 2026-11-03 (earlier captures 2026-11-02 as marked). Prepared for: the founder.
This memo is research, not legal advice; the founder holds no attorney or real estate license.

## What the publisher's terms say

The Department of Tax Administration tables are published by the county GIS Division on <https://data-fairfaxcountygis.opendata.arcgis.com/> (review record, reviewed 2026-11-02). FactLot's five tables are Parcels Data, Legal Data, Assessed Values, Land Data, and Commercial Data. Each dataset's license field says only to review the GIS Division disclaimer, <https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/maps/disclaimer> (read 2026-11-02, NOTES.md):

> Copyright by Fairfax County. Except as provided herein, all rights are reserved. Authorization to reproduce material for internal or personal use by any user of this information is granted by Fairfax County, the copyright owner.

The same page says the layers are "provided as a public resource" and that the county "makes no warranties, expressed or implied". A further paragraph, citing Section 54.1-402, says depictions of improvements or boundaries "shall not be used for the design, modification, or construction of improvements to real property or for flood plain determination." AI use is not addressed. The copyright paragraph grants internal or personal use and reserves every other right. Redistribution in an open registry is not granted.

None of the eight published Tax Administration tables has an owner-of-record column (review record). The owner file is a separate request: `va-fairfax-assessor-request.md`.

## What the statute says

Va. Code § 2.2-3700(B), <https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title2.2/chapter37/section2.2-3700/> (read 2026-11-02), says public records "shall be presumed open" and the chapter "shall be liberally construed".

Va. Code § 2.2-3704(A), <https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title2.2/chapter37/section2.2-3704/> (read 2026-11-02):

> Except as otherwise specifically provided by law, all public records shall be open to citizens of the Commonwealth, representatives of newspapers and magazines with circulation in the Commonwealth, and representatives of radio and television stations broadcasting in or into the Commonwealth during the regular office hours of the custodian of such records.

That grant runs to Virginia citizens and to in-state print and broadcast media. The custodian "may require the requester to provide his name and legal address." A request from a person who is not in that list relies on the county's discretion. The founder's citizenship is not known here. The request file uses the placeholder "[CONFIRM: requester is / is not a Virginia citizen]."

Subsection (F) says the public body "shall provide the requester with a cost estimate if requested." Charges may not exceed actual cost. Subsection (G) requires an electronic database to be produced in a medium the requester names, including email when the county already uses it. Commercial use: silent. Redistribution after receipt: silent.

Va. Code § 58.1-3122.2, <https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/chapter32/section58.1-3122.2/> (read 2026-11-02), says the commissioner of the revenue "may provide remote access, including access through the Internet, to all nonconfidential public records maintained by his office, subject to such limitations as may be imposed by applicable law." Commercial use: silent. Redistribution by a recipient: silent.

## Options

1. Use as a public record.
2. Ask the publisher for a written statement (letter: `va-fairfax-assessor-letter.md`).
3. Do not load.

## Recommendation

Choose option 2 for the five published tables, and do not publish facts from them until the county answers. The disclaimer grants reproduction for internal or personal use and reserves all other rights. The scratch evaluation already run under `--include-unverified` stays inside that internal-use grant. Publication in the open registry does not.

Send the terms letter to the Countywide FOIA Officer, Office of Public Affairs, 12000 Government Center Parkway, Suite 551, Fairfax, VA 22035, FOIA@fairfaxcounty.gov, and to the Department of Tax Administration, Real Estate Division by title (address source <https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/publicaffairs/foia/>, read 2026-11-02). Send the separate owner-file request only after the citizenship placeholder is filled in. Leave `verified` false until the reuse question is answered.

## Risk if wrong

If the copyright reservation controls, publishing derived facts is a use the disclaimer does not authorize. If the reservation reaches only the county's map compilation, waiting delays a daily roll that already lacks owner names. On the owner file, § 2.2-3704(A) gives the statutory right to Virginia citizens and in-state media. A requester outside that list depends on county discretion. Fill the citizenship line before sending.
