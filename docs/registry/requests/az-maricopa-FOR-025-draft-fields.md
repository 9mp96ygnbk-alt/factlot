## The founder must complete

1. `Address, City, State, Zip` and `Address 1`–`4`: replace `[FOUNDER MAILING ADDRESS]`.
2. `Email`: replace `[CONFIRM SENDER EMAIL]`. `Phone` and `Contact Phone Number`: replace `[CONFIRM SENDER PHONE]`. `Fax`: optional.
3. Read every filled value above and correct anything that is not the true intended use (A.R.S.
   § 39-121.03(C) makes a person who misstates the commercial purpose liable for three times the
   charge that would have applied; see the memo).
4. Page 2 certification: sign `Requesters Signature` and write `Date`.
5. Page 3 addendum: write `Date_2`; tick one payment box (they are printed, not form fields): the
   "not to exceed" box (with the amount you choose) or the deposit box (`Amount Agreed to Deposit`,
   `Remaining Balance`) only if the office quotes a fee; sign `Requesters Signature_2` and `Date_3`.
6. Leave the receipt acknowledgement (`Requesters Signature_3`, `Date_4`) blank until the records are delivered.
7. Send the signed form to the Custodian of Records, Maricopa County Assessor's Office, with the
   cover letter [`az-maricopa-assessor-letter.md`](az-maricopa-assessor-letter.md). The office address
   and email in that letter come from the review record and were not re-read on 2026-11-03.
