## Statewide sources checked first

- **New York.** ORPTS publishes every non-NYC final assessment roll as one Open NY dataset, `7vem-aaz7`
  (4.7 million parcels, roll years 2021 to 2025, annual, last updated 2026-04-24), with owner names
  and mailing addresses, SWIS and print key, property class, full market value and assessed values.
  No building area or year built. Terms: OPEN-NY (shared with `ny-dos-entities`). Source:
  [ny-orpts-rolls.md](../sources/ny-orpts-rolls.md).
- **New Jersey.** The Division of Taxation's MOD-IV tax list reaches the public through NJOGIS's
  statewide parcels joined with MOD-IV (feature layer of 3,481,240 parcels; county file
  geodatabases and shapefiles; a statewide MOD-IV table). Owner names are redacted in every public
  download and service under Daniel's Law (P.L. 2020, c. 125), and there is no licence. Source:
  [nj-modiv-parcels.md](../sources/nj-modiv-parcels.md).
- **Connecticut.** OPM's GIS Office publishes the towns' CAMA data and parcels each year on
  `data.ct.gov` (2026 edition `ibe8-9i3q`, 1,359,259 rows, all 169 towns) with owner names. Connecticut
  replaced its counties with nine planning regions in 2022; the Capitol Planning Region (FIPS
  09110, 994,115 residents) has 38 towns (Hartford County: Avon, Berlin, Bloomfield, Canton, East
  Granby, East Hartford, East Windsor, Enfield, Farmington, Glastonbury, Granby, Hartford,
  Manchester, Marlborough, New Britain, Newington, Plainville, Rocky Hill, Simsbury, South Windsor,
  Southington, Suffield, West Hartford, Wethersfield, Windsor, Windsor Locks; Tolland County: Andover,
  Bolton, Columbia, Coventry, Ellington, Hebron, Mansfield, Somers, Stafford, Tolland, Vernon,
  Willington). The parcel source covers all 38 towns in the 2026, 2025 and 2024 editions. Source:
  [ct-parcel-cama.md](../sources/ct-parcel-cama.md).

## Owner names and statutes limiting commercial use

| State | Owner names in the public file                                                                | Statute restricting commercial use of assessment lists?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| NY    | Yes, every county (persons and companies; persons are dropped at ingest)                      | None found. Real Property Tax Law §516 requires the roll to be filed for public inspection and restricts nothing. Public Officers Law §89(2)(b)(iii) treats "sale or release of lists of names and addresses if such lists would be used for solicitation or fund-raising purposes" as an unwarranted invasion of privacy (a ground to withhold under FOIL, not a limit on published open data).         |
| NJ    | No: redacted by NJOGIS in all public downloads and services (Daniel's Law, P.L. 2020, c. 125) | No limit on commercial use of the tax list as such found (OPRA, N.J.S.A. 47:1A-5, only sets timing and fees for commercial requests). Statute text read only through secondary summaries; counsel should confirm. |
| CT    | Yes (owner and co-owner, mailing address)                                                     | None found. C.G.S. §12-55 (grand list open to public inspection) and §7-100l (data submitted to COGs and OPM) restrict nothing; C.G.S. §1-217 (residential addresses of certain public officials) requires towns to withhold protected persons' addresses, so some owners are suppressed. Text of §1-217 read only through case annotations.                                                             |
