## Statewide findings

- **Utah (UGRC).** The statewide LIR layers cover 29 counties under CC BY 4.0 with market values, acreage, building square footage and year built, but no owners. One review and one
  adapter ([`ut_ugrc.py`](../../../python/src/factlot/registry/adapters/ut_ugrc.py)) serve every county; the other 27 counties are a config line each. This is the cleanest licence in the region.
- **Washington (WaTech).** The statewide parcel layer (`wa-state-parcels`) covers all 39 counties (including gated King) with land use code and land and building values but no owners,
  area or year; it is gated only on the state's data disclaimer ("commercial use and licensing of state data is prohibited"). Its ownership edition is not public.
- **Washington, RCW 42.56.070.** King is gated on a checkbox acknowledgement. Snohomish states the same representation as a condition of access ("By proceeding and accessing this data, you agree
  and represent ..."). Pierce states nothing and its Data Mart has no owner or taxpayer table, so there is no list of individuals; that is why Pierce is verified and the other two are not.
- **Arizona.** Pima's terms are not the same document as Maricopa's, but the same statute (A.R.S. 39-121.03) governs both; Pima adds a county ownership claim and a "no public-facing application"
  clause, and its Assessor site forbids all robots.
- **Oregon.** Metro's RLIS tax lots cover Multnomah, Washington and Clackamas Counties (ownership excluded); Oregon DOR property classes (`2xx`, `3xx`, `7xx`) are published in the layer metadata.
  Multnomah sells owners.
- **Hawaii.** Honolulu publishes owners and values weekly but no tax class; Hawaii has no free statewide bulk business file (a four-year-old City copy exists).
