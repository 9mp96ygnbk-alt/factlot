# Tier 2 southeast: Wake NC; Gwinnett, Cobb, DeKalb GA; Shelby, Davidson TN; Jefferson KY; Oklahoma County and Tulsa OK

Researched 2026-11-03. Rules and method: [README.md](README.md) and [EXPANSION-BRIEF.md](../EXPANSION-BRIEF.md). Every
count below is from a real run on a real file (loaded into a scratch registry) or, where marked "service count", from
the publisher's own query service without a download. Nothing was bought, signed, requested or registered.

**Result:** three counties verified and loaded (Wake, Davidson, DeKalb: 34,096 parcels in scope across B, F1 and F2); six
gated (Gwinnett, Cobb, Shelby, Jefferson KY, Oklahoma County, Tulsa); the three new entity registries (TN, KY, OK) are
all paid, and the two Tier 1 gates (NC, GA) stand.

linking in this region waits on the founder's purchase decisions. Registered agents and officers are natural persons in
all of them and must not be stored.
