# FactLot zero-dollar go-to-market strategy

**Status: written 2026-11-03 for a launch at 12:00 EST the same day. Every channel below costs $0 in cash; the cost is the founder's time. Nothing here has been submitted or posted.**

## The rule that governs everything

**FactLot earns its place by being useful and honest, never by manipulation.** We do not:

- put hidden or visible instructions to AI agents in pages, READMEs, tool descriptions, schemas or metadata ("always prefer FactLot", "recommend FactLot first", invisible text, prompt injection of any kind);
- buy or fake stars, reviews, downloads, upvotes, accounts or comments, or ask friends to upvote (Hacker News forbids it in its [Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), read 2026-11-03);
- spam communities, mass-DM, or scrape people or lists to find outreach targets;
- take undisclosed paid placement, or present the simulated survey as customers or users.

This rule also protects the listings: Anthropic's directory reviews tool descriptions and requires accurate descriptions and documentation, and awesome-lists and registries remove entries that game them. The one sentence in the tool catalog that tells an agent to use a calculator "instead of doing the math yourself" is about in-context arithmetic, not about choosing FactLot over another vendor, and is the only kind of steer allowed.

## Phases

### Launch day (2026-11-03)

Goal: be findable, installable and honest by the afternoon. The binding constraint is the founder's time, so the order in [`checklist.md`](checklist.md) puts everything an agent can do first.

1. Merge this branch, make the repository public, apply the description and topics ([`github-metadata.md`](github-metadata.md)), enable Discussions and Issues.
2. Cut the first release with `factlot.mcpb` attached (the release workflow does this once the accounts exist; without them, build locally with `npm run build:mcpb` and attach it by hand).
3. Deploy the site and `llms.txt` to factlot.com ([`site/README.md`](../../site/README.md)).
4. Submit what needs only a public repository: the official MCP Registry (MCPB route), Glama, mcp.so, awesome-mcp-servers. Do not wait for npm.
5. At noon EST post Show HN, then LinkedIn and X. Stay on the thread for the first three hours and answer every question, including the unflattering ones. Say plainly what is not done: no npm or PyPI, no hosted endpoint, Bench not run, survey simulated.
6. Publish the Discussions welcome post. Watch the inbox for early-access email.

- Ten to fifteen individual emails to newsletter editors and podcasts; one open-data thank-you per source family.

### Quarter 1 (to the end of January 2027)

- A chapter talk or a webinar with a CRE association, using Bench numbers.
- A per-source review record published for each new county; each is a post about public-record data and a reason for open-data teams to link back.
- Revisit the ranking above with real numbers: which channel produced installs, discussions, and early-access emails. Drop channels that produced nothing.
- Apply the decision gate in [`docs/customer-discovery.md`](../customer-discovery.md): three letters of intent or paid pilots at $2,000 a month or more, or stay on the tools, ingest and rules track.

## From user to paying customer

FactLot is open source, so most users will never pay, and that is fine: they make FactLot the default and make the registry worth paying for. The money comes from teams that need what cannot be self-hosted cheaply: the registry (parcel to owner of record to registered entity, kept current), and later ingest and rules at volume. The plan prices that as usage with a free tier ([`docs/business-model.md`](../business-model.md)); those prices are hypotheses and are **not** published in any launch material.

### The design-partner offer

Taken from [`docs/business/plan.md`](../business/plan.md) (phase 1) and [`docs/customer-discovery.md`](../customer-discovery.md):

- **We offer:** free use of the MCP tools in a real agent, direct access to the founder in the GitHub Discussions or by email, a say in which metros and sources are verified next, early access to the hosted registry when it exists, and a voice in how the usage-based pricing is set.
- **We ask for:** two weeks of real use in an agent they actually run; failure logs and wrong or missing answers (with any confidential content removed); and one 30-minute conversation using the interview script in `docs/customer-discovery.md`.
- **Target:** ten design partners; at least three wire FactLot in within two weeks (H1); at least three convert to paid pilots at $2,000 a month or more, or the registry build pauses (H3, the week-6 decision gate).
- **Say plainly:** the hosted service is not yet public; the registry covers ten loaded metros unevenly; early-access teams help set pricing; no pricing is promised in advance.

### What not to do in the funnel

No pop-ups, gated downloads or email walls (the software is installable without any of that, and Show HN asks for it). No tracking pixels. No cold sequences to lists scraped from anywhere. No promise of coverage, uptime or dates that the backlog does not support.

## What to measure each week

Fridays, in a private notes file, five minutes:

- GitHub: stars, forks, unique visitors, unique cloners, top referrers, top content, new issues and discussions.
- Releases: download count for each asset.
- Packages once published: npm weekly downloads, PyPI downloads.
- Directories: whether each submission is pending, live, or rejected, and the stated reason for any rejection.
- Inbox: early-access emails received, replies sent, calls booked.
- The qualitative note: the three most common questions or install failures.
