Privacy · FactLot

        FactLot [https://factlot.com/]

          Privacy

          Effective 3 November 2026.

            FactLot collects as little as it can. This page covers factlot.com,
            the hosted API at api.factlot.com, and paid plans. The open-source
            packages you run yourself make no network calls and send FactLot
            nothing.

          The website

            factlot.com sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and uses no analytics
            or tracking. Cloudflare, which hosts it, processes your IP address
            to deliver the page and protect it from abuse.

          The hosted API

              Not logged: request bodies, tool arguments,
              results, and headers. Documents you send are processed in memory
              to answer the request and are not stored.

              Metered: for each request, the time, path, plan
              tier, response status, and the first 16 characters of a one-way
              hash of your API key (never the key). This is kept for usage
              reporting and abuse prevention.

              Rate limits: free-tier requests are counted per
              IP address for one minute, in memory, then discarded.

          Paid plans

            Stripe processes payments; FactLot never sees your card number.
            FactLot stores your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your plan,
            and a SHA-256 hash of your API key. Stripe's privacy policy covers
            what Stripe collects.

          Registry data

            The FactLot Registry holds public records about commercial property.
            It stores owner names only for organisations and never for natural
            persons. If you believe a record names you as a person, or is wrong,
            email contact@factlot.com and we will review and remove it.

          Email

            If you write to contact@factlot.com, we keep your message to answer
            it. Please do not send confidential leases or personal data by
            email.

          Sharing and your rights

            FactLot does not sell or share personal data, and does not use your
            API requests to train models. You can ask what we hold about you, or
            ask us to delete it, at contact@factlot.com. We will update this
            page and its date when practices change.
