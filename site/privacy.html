Privacy

Effective 7 November 2026. The revision history is at the end.

FactLot collects as little as it can. This notice covers factlot.com, the hosted API and MCP server at api.factlot.com, and paid plans. The open-source packages you run yourself make no network calls and send FactLot nothing.

Who we are

factlot.com and api.factlot.com are run by Robert Stovall, a sole proprietor in Texas, USA, doing business as FactLot ("FactLot", "we"). FactLot decides how the information described here is used.

Email: contact@factlot.com
Postal address: pending.

What we collect

The website

factlot.com sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. Cloudflare, which hosts it, processes your IP address, browser user agent and the page you ask for, to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse. Cloudflare may keep request logs under its own privacy policy; FactLot keeps no access log of its own.

The hosted API

  • Usage records. For each request we record the time, the API key ID (the first 16 characters of a SHA-256 hash of the key, never the key itself, or "anonymous" when there is no key), the plan tier, the path, the HTTP method and the response status code. No IP address is recorded. These records are kept in a Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine dataset.
  • Rate-limit counters. To enforce the per-minute limit, requests are counted per IP address on the Free plan and per key on paid plans, for the current one-minute window only.
  • Lookup quota counters. To enforce registry lookup quotas, lookups are counted for the current day on the Free plan, keyed by a hash of the IP address (for IPv6, of its /64 prefix), never the address itself, and per subscription for the current month on paid plans. Days and months are UTC. A counter is deleted when its window ends.
  • Error logs. When a request fails, the Worker logs only the error's name (for example TypeError), never its message or any request content.
  • Key records. For each paid key we store a SHA-256 hash of the key, the plan, the key's status, the Stripe customer and subscription IDs, the subscription status, the date the key was created, the time of the last subscription check, the time the key was revoked (revoked_at), whether a refund or payment dispute has put the key on hold, and the time a payment last failed (payment_failed_at).

Billing

Stripe runs checkout and billing and collects what it needs to take payment: your email address, name, card or bank details, billing address, and a tax ID if checkout asks for one. Stripe handles these under its own privacy policy. In Stripe, FactLot can see your email, name and billing address as you entered them, your subscription status, your invoices, and your card's brand and last four digits. FactLot never sees or stores your full card number.

Email

If you write to contact@factlot.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards it to the operator's mailbox. Please do not send confidential leases or personal data by email.

GitHub

Issues and Discussions on GitHub are public and covered by GitHub's privacy statement. Anything you post there can be read by anyone.

What we do not collect

  • Request bodies, tool inputs and outputs, and documents. Document text is processed in memory to answer the request and is not stored or logged.
  • Request headers, other than reading the key to authorise the request.
  • IP addresses in usage records.
  • Cookies, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers.
  • Natural-person names in the registry. The registry holds commercial property and the organisations that own it. It is built to keep owners that are organisations and to exclude natural persons' names, and owner records a source withheld are never recovered or inferred. If a record names you as a person, or is wrong, email contact@factlot.com and we will remove or correct it.

We do not use your requests or documents to train models, and we do not build profiles of you.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for: answering requests, checking keys, applying your plan. This is needed to perform our agreement with you.
  • To keep the service working and fair: rate limits, quotas, abuse prevention and fixing errors. This is our legitimate interest in running a reliable service.
  • To bill you and keep tax records: needed to perform our agreement and required by law.
  • To answer you when you write to us, and to send notices about your plan, these terms or a security problem.
  • To plan capacity: counting requests by tool path, plan and status, with no IP address.

We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising, and make no automated decisions about you with legal or similar effects.

How long we keep it

What Kept for

Cloudflare request logs Under Cloudflare's own policy

Your rights

Anyone can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy (access);
  • correct it (correction);
  • delete it (deletion);
  • give it to you in a portable, machine-readable form (portability); and
  • stop using it for a purpose you object to.

We give everyone the rights above. Where the California Consumer Privacy Act or the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act applies, they include the rights that law sets out.

California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act): the rights to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to delete it; to correct it; to opt out of its sale or sharing; and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use sensitive personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of or limit.

Texas Data Privacy and Security Act: the rights to confirm whether we process your personal data and to access it; to correct it; to delete it; to get a portable copy; and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects. We do none of those three. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our answer with the word "Appeal"; we answer an appeal within 60 days. If you are not satisfied with that answer, you can contact the Texas Attorney General.

We will not treat you differently, charge you more or give you a worse service for using any of these rights.

How to use your rights

Email contact@factlot.com from the address on your account, or include your API key ID from a response or your Stripe receipt. Never send us the key itself. We confirm who you are by replying to that address, answer within 45 days (once extendable by another 45 days if we tell you why), and charge nothing. An authorised agent may ask for you with your written permission.

Security

  • API keys are random, shown once, and stored only as a SHA-256 hash.
  • Every connection to factlot.com and api.factlot.com is encrypted with TLS.
  • We keep as little as possible: no request content, no documents, no IP addresses in usage records, and no card numbers.
  • Stripe handles all card data.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you as the law requires. To report a vulnerability, see SECURITY.md.

Children

FactLot is a business service for people aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

International use

FactLot is run from the United States. Cloudflare serves requests from the data centre nearest you on its global network, and Cloudflare, Stripe and GitHub are United States companies. If you use FactLot from outside the United States, your information is processed in the United States.
