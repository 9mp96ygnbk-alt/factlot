What it computes

Net effective rent (NER) is the average annual base rent per square foot a landlord keeps over a lease term after free rent and the landlord's leasing costs. The calculator returns three figures in dollars per square foot per year: effective rent (the average rent actually paid after abatement), straight-line NER (effective rent less the tenant improvement allowance, leasing commissions and other concessions, spread evenly over the term), and discounted NER (the level rent with the same present value as the landlord's net cash flows at a stated discount rate).

Method and conventions

The base-rent schedule is built exactly as calc_rent_schedule builds it. Lease month 1 starts on commencement_date, and each later month starts on the same day of the month, clamped to month end. The annual rate for month m uses k = floor((m − 1) ÷ every_months) completed bumps: a percent escalation gives starting_rate × (1 + rate)^k and a fixed escalation gives starting_rate + amount × k. Monthly rent is rate × area_sf ÷ 12, rounded to the cent. Each month listed in abated_months has zero rent due.

The term in years is term_months ÷ 12. Landlord costs are ti_allowance_psf × area_sf + leasing_commissions + other_concessions, all paid at commencement. Effective rent is total rent due ÷ area ÷ years. Straight-line NER is (total rent due − landlord costs) ÷ area ÷ years.

For discounted NER, rent is paid monthly in advance, so month 1 has a discount factor of 1. The monthly rate is r = discount_rate_annual ÷ 12 (a nominal rate, not an effective one). The present value of rent is Σ rent_due_t ÷ (1 + r)^t for t = 0 … n − 1. The level monthly rent is (PV of rent − landlord costs) ÷ Σ (1 + r)^−t, and discounted NER is that amount × 12 ÷ area. Landlord costs are not discounted because they fall at time zero.

Practitioners differ on several points. Some quote only straight-line NER and others only a discounted figure; FactLot returns both, and discount_rate_annual of 0 makes them equal (the result then carries a warning). Some models pay rent in arrears or convert an effective annual rate to a monthly rate; FactLot always discounts in advance at annual ÷ 12, so an effective annual rate needs converting to its nominal equivalent before it is passed. Some analyses treat free rent as a cost line; FactLot reduces rent due in the abated months instead, so free rent is counted once. Some NER figures include expense recoveries; this one uses base rent only. Escalations run on lease-month anniversaries counted from commencement, not on calendar dates. Optional round_rate_to_cents rounds each step's annual rate to cents, as many lease rent tables do.

Setting schedule.unit to kw, mw, acre, unit, key, room or space relabels the results (for example dollars per kW per year) without changing any number.

Inputs that matter

  • schedule.starting_rent_psf_annual: year-one base rent in dollars per square foot per year, not per month.
  • schedule.term_months: lease term in months; it sets both the schedule length and the years divisor.
  • schedule.area_sf: rentable square feet (or the quantity in unit); totals are divided by it.
  • schedule.escalation: none, percent (compounding) or fixed_psf, with every_months between bumps.
  • schedule.abated_months: fully free months, counted from 1 at commencement.
  • ti_allowance_psf: landlord-funded improvement allowance per square foot, paid once at commencement.
  • leasing_commissions: total commissions paid by the landlord, in dollars.
  • other_concessions: other landlord payments at commencement, such as a moving allowance or lease buyout.
  • discount_rate_annual: annual rate for discounted NER; 0 gives the straight-line figure.

Limits

  • Base rent only. Operating expense recoveries, expense stops and percentage rent are not part of the result.
  • Abatement is whole months at 100%. Half-rent periods or partial-month abatement are not supported.
  • All landlord costs are paid at commencement. Allowances or commissions paid in installments, and downtime before commencement, are not modeled.
  • One escalation pattern applies to the whole term. Irregular step tables with hand-set rates cannot be entered.
  • Rent is not prorated to calendar months. A mid-month commencement produces a warning, and any proration happens outside the result.
  • The tenant's own costs, renewal options and termination options are not considered. The term is at most 1,200 months.
