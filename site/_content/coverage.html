Where FactLot holds whole-county commercial property data, and how far it has been checked. Grey means not usable yet: not reviewed, not loaded, waiting on publisher terms, a sample only, or too old.

What the colors mean

  • Hosted lookups. Served by the hosted API at api.factlot.com on every plan, metered by plan, including the free tier. Call registry_coverage to list what is hosted. DC's data is also published as an open sample database (CC BY 4.0) you can run with the FactLot MCP server.
  • Audited, not yet hosted. The whole county's commercial roll is loaded from a source whose terms we recorded and verified, and an audit re-read the publisher's archived file and found the registry holds exactly its in-scope parcels, with no violations.
  • Loaded, not yet audited. The whole county is loaded from a verified source, with a per-county count and a data date in the review record, but the audit has not covered it yet.
  • Not usable yet. Everything else, including statewide loads with no per-county count, partial or sample loads, sources whose terms are still open, and data more than {{COV_MAX_AGE}} days old.

Hosted lookups are open now for the counties marked hosted: Maryland, the District of Columbia, Juab and Summit counties in Utah, and sixteen Texas counties (Harris, Dallas, Travis, Collin, Denton, Williamson, Cameron, Nueces, Galveston, Jefferson, Brazos, Midland, Ector, Grayson, Guadalupe and Kaufman). Tarrant County is not served by the hosted registry while the publisher's terms for AI use are unclear. To ask for another county, email us.

Method and sources
  • Every county is keyed by its 5-digit Census FIPS code. Counts are quoted from the registry's review records and checked by the generator; nothing is estimated.
  • The registry is built to keep owners that are organisations and to exclude natural persons' names. If a record names you as a person, or is wrong, email contact@factlot.com and we will remove or correct it.
  • The freshness limit is {{COV_MAX_AGE}} days before {{COV_ASOF}}, the latest source review date.
  • Machine-readable file: /coverage/counties.json. Full per-source terms and records: registry coverage.
  • Boundaries: US Census Bureau cartographic boundary files (public domain), via us-atlas.
