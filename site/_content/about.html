FactLot is an open-source fact layer for commercial real estate software and AI agents: deterministic calculators with audit trails, stable IDs for parcels, entities and buildings, and public-record facts that each cite their source and its terms of use.

Who runs it

FactLot is run by {{OPERATOR}}. It is run by one person. This is the same operator named in the privacy notice and the terms.

Robert Stovall also publishes the sister site FactLease, a dated record of commercial asking rents in Greater Houston and San Antonio. Some addresses of an earlier version of factlot.com, which carried those rent pages, now redirect there.

What FactLot is not

FactLot is not a real estate brokerage and is not licensed to broker real estate. It does not list property for sale or lease and does not represent buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants or property owners. FactLot is software: it is not a property tax consultant and does not prepare, file or advise on protests. Nothing here is an appraisal or an opinion of value, or legal, tax or investment advice. Calculator results follow stated conventions; whether they fit a deal is for you and your advisers.

Data and names

Commercial property only. FactLot shows owners of record only when they are organisations; the registry is built to exclude natural persons' names. If a record names you as a person, or is wrong, email contact@factlot.com and it will be removed or corrected.
