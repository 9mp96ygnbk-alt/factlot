Using FactLot without an AI assistant

Everything here works in a browser, with no AI client, account or key.

  • This page looks up a commercial property by address and shows a plain summary first: the address, the owner of record when it is an organisation (or why there is none), the roll values with their year and date, building and land facts, and the source of each. "Copy as text" puts the same summary on your clipboard for an email.
  • A lookup page is the same lookup as a web address, with no script: factlot.com/lookup/?q=1000+Louisiana+St&state=TX answers a page of cited facts that can be bookmarked or sent. It is for a person's browser or a program: robots.txt asks crawlers, and assistants that follow it, not to fetch lookup addresses, so an assistant should use the connector or the API. A search is one lookup and a record another, as here, and a record that has a property page usually goes to that page, uncounted. {{PLAN_LOOKUP_RULE}} Look up by address.
  • The property sheet is a one-page PDF of the same record, with every source, its date and its licence or attribution, to attach or print. It is made when you ask for it, uses one of the free tier's 3 reports a month and one lookup, and its download link works for 24 hours. On the free tier it shows the current roll year; a Pro or Team key adds the earlier years the registry holds.
  • A property page is a public web page for one parcel, under factlot.com/property/, for parcels whose sources allow their data to be republished. It shows the same cited facts for the current roll year and can be bookmarked or shared. When a parcel has one, the summary links it.
  • The calculators each have a worked example on the calculators page, with every input and step shown.
