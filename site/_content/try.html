The lookup above is the same registry_search_address and registry_get that your agent calls over MCP, sent from your browser to factlot.com/v1/registry/…. Your address search goes to FactLot's API and is counted against your IP address's free lookups; nothing is stored in your browser and no cookie is set. On the free tier the record's value history is the current roll year only; a Pro or Team key returns every year (plans).
