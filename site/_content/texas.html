Tools

  • registry_get: a parcel, legal entity or building by FactLot ID, with its status, current cited facts and ownership links. A parcel's value history by tax year is every year with a Pro or Team key and the current roll year without one.
  • registry_tx_equity_candidates (and its batch form, which is for Pro and Team): candidate comparables for a subject parcel under a stated default rule, returned in the input shape of the calculator below for a person to review and edit. One parcel per call is within the free lookups, and the subject it returns carries its value history by tax year: every year with a Pro or Team key, the current roll year without one.
  • calc_tx_unequal_appraisal: the arithmetic of Tax Code §41.43(b)(3) and §42.26(a)(3) over comparables the person chose, with every step shown.
  • rules_tx_property_tax_protest_deadline: the protest deadline computed from the statute text, with citations. See the Texas property tax protest deadline page.
  • report_create kinds tx_protest_deadline (a calendar or PDF of the deadline) and tx_unequal_appraisal (an evidence packet as a PDF, or the Texas workbook, an XLSX with live Excel formulas beside FactLot's numbers), built by code with no model.

Registry lookups and reports are metered by plan; see pricing.

What this is and is not

These outputs are not an appraisal or an opinion of value (Occupations Code §1103.003(1)), not legal or tax advice, and not a substitute for the advice of an attorney. They do not say whether to protest, what value to put forward, or how a hearing may go. FactLot does not file protests, sign agent designations under §1.111, or contact appraisal districts. Filing is done through the appraisal district's own process, by the owner or by a property tax consultant registered with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation, or an attorney.
