Plans also differ in request rate, registry lookups, reports, keys and support.

  • Full multi-year value history. On the free tier, registry_get returns a parcel's market and appraised value for the current roll year only, and include_history returns only the current facts; the subject in a registry_tx_equity_candidates answer is cut the same way. The answer says what was left out and links here. With a Pro or Team key they return every tax year and every snapshot.
  • Earlier dates and named roll years. registry_get with as_of returns a record as it stood on an earlier date, and registry_tx_equity_candidates with tax_year lists candidates from the roll year you name. Without a Pro or Team key both answer PlanRequired and count nothing; the same calls without those arguments stay within the free daily lookups.
  • An owner's whole portfolio. On the free tier registry_owned_by returns an owner's first three parcels and says how many were left out; with a Pro or Team key it returns every parcel.
  • Batch lookup for many parcels at once. registry_lookup_batch takes up to 50 parcels in the hosted regions in one call, each by URN, account number or address, and returns a cited row per parcel: account, category, use code, area, year built, current-year value and owner of record. registry_tx_equity_candidates_batch takes up to 50 Texas parcels for unequal-appraisal candidates. Without a Pro or Team key they answer PlanRequired and count nothing; you can call the single-parcel tools (registry_get, registry_resolve_parcel, registry_search_address, registry_tx_equity_candidates) once per parcel within the free daily lookups. With a key, each parcel counts as its single call would.

What stays free

  • Every calculator, ID, fact, rules and document tool, including the batch forms of the CAM reconciliation and the Texas protest deadline tools.
  • registry_coverage, and registry_get, registry_resolve_parcel, the address, entity and owner searches, within 25 registry lookups a day per IP address.
  • registry_tx_equity_candidates, one parcel per call, within the same free lookups, from the newest roll year with values. Without tax_year the candidates and their calculator input are the same on every plan; the subject's value history is the current roll year on the free tier.
  • 3 reports a month per IP address, including the Texas evidence packet and workbook.

What counts as a registry lookup

  • A lookup is one answered call to a registry_* tool: a record, a resolved ID, owners or search results, and a call inside loaded coverage that finds nothing still counts. Free: registry_coverage, input errors, misses outside loaded coverage (county, state, entity registry or ID system not loaded) and calls the registry could not answer.
  • A registry batch tool needs one lookup per subject or item left in the allowance to run (a batch larger than what remains is refused whole). registry_tx_equity_candidates_batch counts one lookup per answered subject, and subjects with an error are given back. registry_lookup_batch counts each item as the single call that identifies its parcel would count (registry_resolve_parcel for an account or a URN, registry_search_address for an address): a miss inside loaded coverage counts, a URN not in the registry included; an item with an error and a miss outside loaded coverage are given back. One MCP request may make at most 500 registry service calls (a registry_lookup_batch item counts 2, a registry_tx_equity_candidates_batch subject 1); a batch call past that is refused whole and counts nothing. Both are part of Pro and Team.
  • Without a key, 25 lookups a day per IP address (stored only as a hash; IPv6 per /64), counted per UTC day and reset at 00:00 UTC.
  • With a key, lookups count per subscription per calendar month (UTC), shared by all of its keys; requests a minute count per key.
  • At a cap, registry calls answer 429 RegistryQuotaExceeded until the reset, other tools keep working, and nothing extra is billed.

The same rules are served as JSON at api.factlot.com/v1/plans.

Billing, cancelling and refunds

Checkout is by Stripe; prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes. The Start buttons open Stripe Checkout. Your API key is shown once on the page Stripe returns you to; FactLot stores only a hash of it, and a new key can take up to a minute to start working.

Cancel any time at factlot.com/account, in the Stripe billing portal, or by email to contact@factlot.com with the subject "Cancel" from your account email. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and your keys work to the end of the period you have paid for (a month, or a year on Pro yearly). There are no refunds for a partial month, or a partial year on yearly billing; if you are charged in error, for example twice, the wrong amount, or after you cancelled, email us and we will correct it. See the terms and the privacy notice.

Agents can start checkout with POST https://api.factlot.com/v1/billing/checkout, hand the link to their human, and collect the key from the claim_url it returns.
